Pro Photo Editing & Prompting Tips

Master lighting syntax, aspect ratios, and color grading keywords.

💡

1. Subject First Syntax

Always place your core subject in the first 5 words. Generators pay the highest weight to initial tokens. Example: "A cyborg cyber artist, high contrast..." works better than long intros.

📸

2. Lens & Focal Length Magic

Specify focal lengths: 85mm for flattering portraits without distortion, 24mm for dynamic environmental angles, and 100mm Macro for intricate detail rendering.

🎨

3. Color Grading Terms

Instead of generic "colorful", use terms like "Kodak Portra 400 aesthetic", "Teal & Orange split toning", or "Cinematic volumetric haze" for exact color profiles.

✨

4. Negative Prompt Secrets

Avoid unwanted glitches by including keywords like: "deformed limbs, extra fingers, oversaturated glow, blur, low quality, artifacts, watermark" in your negative prompt settings.
